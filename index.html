Bluetooth Car Controller

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bluetooth Car Controller (“the App”) handles information when you use it to connect your Android device to compatible Bluetooth serial modules, such as the HC-05, and control Arduino-based RC car projects.

1. Information the App accesses

  • Bluetooth and nearby-device access: The App uses Bluetooth permissions to discover or list paired Bluetooth devices, connect to a device you select, and send the control commands you choose (for example, forward, reverse, left, right, stop, and light commands).
  • Device name and Bluetooth address: The App may display the name and Bluetooth address of paired devices so you can identify the device you want to connect to.
  • Location permission on supported Android versions: Android versions and Bluetooth APIs may require location permission for Bluetooth scanning/discovery. The App does not use this permission to determine, collect, store, or transmit your physical location. You can manage permissions in Android Settings; denying a required permission may prevent Bluetooth discovery or connection from working.
  • Command profiles: If you create or edit command profiles, those settings may be handled by the App on your device to configure the commands sent to your car. This policy assumes profiles remain on your device and are not uploaded to a server.

2. How information is used

The App uses Bluetooth-related information only to show available paired devices, establish a Bluetooth connection, and send your selected control commands to the connected hardware. The App is a local controller and does not require you to create an account.

3. Data collection, sharing, and advertising

The App is designed not to send personal information or Bluetooth device details to the developer or to a remote server. It does not include advertising or analytics in the version this policy describes. The App does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Your Android operating system, Bluetooth hardware, or other apps on your device may process information under their own privacy practices. If you connect to third-party hardware, the way that hardware uses commands or other information is outside the App developer’s control.

4. Data storage and retention

The App does not maintain a developer-hosted account or cloud database. Bluetooth commands are sent directly from your phone to the selected Bluetooth device. Any command-profile settings are expected to remain locally on your device. You can remove paired devices through Android Bluetooth settings and uninstall the App to remove its app-specific data, subject to Android’s normal device behavior and backups.

5. Security

The App communicates with compatible hardware over Bluetooth. Bluetooth is a short-range wireless connection and should only be paired with devices you trust. Keep your phone and connected hardware secure, and do not operate a motorized RC car where it could injure someone or cause damage.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-purpose controller for electronics and hobby projects and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the App does not intentionally collect personal information, it does not knowingly maintain such information about children.

7. Your choices

You can manage Bluetooth and other app permissions through Android Settings, disconnect from a device, remove paired devices, or uninstall the App. Some features may not work if required Bluetooth permissions are disabled.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the App’s features, permissions, or data practices change. The revised version will be published at the same policy URL with an updated effective date.
